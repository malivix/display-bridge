font. Initial playback action is Cancel, not Play; initial observation is Not sure. Closing or
Escape must never imply playback or hearing. Recalculate keyboard traversal before modal entry.

shared build inventory. No backend or playback-policy change.

past response. Never dispatch audio-test in demo mode. Verify Largest layout, keyboard/defaults,
cancellation and the two-step synthetic path, plus native checks and staged privacy review.
